Privacy Policy

How MD57 Car Wash & Auto Detailing collects, uses, and protects your information.

Last updated: October 6, 2026

This policy covers the MD57 Car Wash & Auto Detailing queue, booking, and workforce management system ("the System"). It applies to customers who book a service or join the queue, and to staff/admin accounts used to run the shop. Personal information is handled in line with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173); customers agree to this policy when they create an account.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: name, email address, and (for staff/admin accounts) a username and role.
  • Booking details: vehicle plate number, vehicle type, service requested, booking date/time, assigned bay, and queue status.
  • Feedback: ratings and comments you submit after a completed service.
  • Google account information: if you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile picture from Google - we do not receive your Google password.
  • Operational data: for staff accounts, attendance/shift records and job completion history, used to calculate performance rankings shown to admins.

How We Use Your Information

  • To manage your booking, place you in the live queue, and notify you when your vehicle is ready.
  • To send booking confirmations and status updates by email.
  • To estimate wait times and staffing needs using historical booking patterns (no data is sold or used for advertising).
  • To let you leave feedback on completed services, and to show aggregate ratings to shop management.
  • For staff/admin accounts, to track attendance and job performance for internal scheduling and productivity reporting.

Third-Party Services

We use a small number of third-party services to operate the System, each only for the specific purpose below:

  • Google Sign-In: optional alternative to a password for logging in.
  • Email delivery (SMTP): to send booking confirmations and status notifications.
  • Weather data: used to display current conditions and adjust wait-time estimates - this call does not include any of your personal information.

Data Retention

Booking, feedback, and attendance records are kept for as long as needed for queue history, reporting, and service-quality tracking. You may request deletion of your account and associated personal data by contacting us using the details below.

Cookies & Sessions

The System uses a session cookie to keep you signed in. It does not use third-party advertising or tracking cookies.

Your Choices

  • You can request a copy of, or the deletion of, your personal data by contacting us.
  • You can ask us to correct information that is wrong or out of date.
  • You can withdraw your consent at any time; your account is then closed.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to the shop administrator through the contact details listed on the homepage.
